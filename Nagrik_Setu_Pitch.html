🎙️ Nagrik Setu: 7-Minute Pitch Script

⏱️ Minute 1: The Hook & Problem Statement

Slide 1: Title & Introduction

"Hello everyone. Today we are presenting Nagrik Setu (Citizen Bridge) — a unified municipal grievance and public service management platform."

Slide 2: The Problem

"Currently, civic grievance systems in India face three massive bottlenecks:

  • Misrouting: Citizens don't know which department handles their issue, leading to delays.
  • Spam & Duplication: Hundreds of people report the same pothole, clogging the system.
  • Lack of Accountability: Ground engineers lack clear assignment workflows, and citizens have zero visibility into the actual status of their complaints."

⏱️ Minute 2: The Solution

Slide 3: Introducing Nagrik Setu

"Nagrik Setu solves this by removing the guesswork. It is a smart, geo-enabled, role-based platform that connects citizens directly to the right government official.

By leveraging Automated Intelligence, our system instantly analyzes a citizen's complaint in plain text, summarizes it, scores its priority, and routes it to the correct department—without the citizen having to select a category."

⏱️ Minute 3: Key Features & "The Wow Factor"

Slide 4: Smart Automation

"Our standout feature is our 100% Offline Automation Engine. Instead of relying on expensive or unreliable external APIs that can break or cause network delays, we built a robust, lightning-fast heuristic engine directly into the platform:

  • Auto-Categorization: If a user types 'live wire', the system instantly recognizes the danger and assigns it to the Electricity department.
  • Priority Scoring: It detects emergency keywords (like 'fire' or 'accident') and boosts the SLA priority score to a 9 or 10.
  • Duplicate Detection: It scans the database for overlapping keywords in recent complaints to prevent spam.
  • Actionable Summaries: It parses long complaints into one-sentence summaries for busy officials."

⏱️ Minute 4: The 4-Tier Workflow

Slide 5: Role-Based Architecture

"We built a fully functional 4-tier ecosystem:

  1. The Citizen: Logs in, drops a pin on the map, and types their issue.
  2. Higher Official (Department Head): Logs in to view a filtered queue of complaints strictly for their department. They can re-route misassigned tickets or assign them to an engineer via email.
  3. Ground Official (Engineer): Logs in to see only their assigned field tasks. Once fixed, they mark it as 'Resolved'.
  4. System Admin: Gets a birds-eye view of all municipalities with a live GIS heatmap and SLA compliance stats."

⏱️ Minute 5: Technical Architecture

Slide 6: Tech Stack

"We engineered this to be highly scalable and lightweight:

  • Frontend: Pure HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. We avoided heavy frameworks to ensure the portal loads instantly, even on poor 3G mobile networks.
  • Backend & Database: We used Supabase (PostgreSQL).
  • Security: We implemented robust relational tables linking profiles, municipalities, and departments, alongside strict database rules.
  • Mapping: Integrated Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap for precise GPS plotting.
  • Hosting: Seamlessly deployed on Vercel."

⏱️ Minute 6: The Live Demo (Action Steps)

Live Demo Walkthrough
  1. Show Citizen View: "I am logging in as a citizen. I report a 'Huge pothole on Main Street'. Watch as the system automatically categorizes it to 'Roads & Traffic' and gives it a priority score."
  2. Show Higher Official View: "Now, I log out and log in as a Higher Official for the Roads department. I see the ticket. I click 'Assign Engineer' and enter an email."
  3. Show Ground Official View: "Finally, the ground engineer logs in. They see the ticket, fix the issue, and click 'Mark Resolved'."
  4. Show Admin Heatmap: "Here is the admin dashboard plotting these issues on a live map."

⏱️ Minute 7: Future Scope & Conclusion

Slide 7: What's Next?

"In the future, we plan to add:

  1. Native mobile apps with camera integration for photo proof.
  2. WhatsApp Bot integration so citizens can file complaints via text.
  3. Regional language translation.

Conclusion:
"With Nagrik Setu, we aren't just logging complaints; we are bridging the gap between citizens and the government, ensuring that every voice is heard, routed, and resolved efficiently. Thank you!"

💡 Tips for the Q&A Session (Judge Questions)

  • "What if your automation categorizes it wrong?"
    Answer: "We built a fail-safe. Higher officials have a 'Forward Dept' button. If an issue lands in Water Supply but belongs in Roads, the official clicks one button, and it instantly bounces to the correct department's queue."

  • "How do you handle security and fake logins?"
    Answer: "We implemented a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) system. Ground engineers can only see their assigned tasks, and department heads can only see their department's queue."

  • "Why didn't you use a framework like React?"
    Answer: "We wanted maximum performance and zero bloat. By using Vanilla JS and the Supabase CDN, the application is incredibly lightweight, which is crucial for citizens accessing it on low-end smartphones."
